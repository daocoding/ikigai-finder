# Reference — Interest Domains

Use this in Stage 3 ([`../method/03-love.md`](../method/03-love.md)) as a controlled vocabulary for naming the *fields* that pull the user. 100 domains; the 12 groups below are a navigational map (the official Japanese appendix is a flat 100-name list with no groups). Anchor, not cage — blends ("the psychology of money") are fine and often the most alive.

Official source: Yagi Jinpei, 『世界一やさしい「やりたいこと」の見つけ方』巻末特典「例100リスト」, 情熱の例100リスト. EN and ZH are faithful translations, updated 2026-11-11. IDs 1–100 are unchanged. All 100 names are now assigned to a group (the previous 18 holes have been filled).

## Quick map — 12 groups

- **Nature & Environment** (自然与环境) — Animals, Flowers, Agriculture, Forestry, Space, Nature, Outdoors
- **Technology** (科技) — Robots, IT, Computers, Electrical Equipment, Electricity, Video
- **Creative Arts** (创意艺术) — Art, Photography, Product Design, Graphic Design, Music, Song, Instruments, Performing Arts
- **Entertainment & Media** (娱乐与媒体) — Events, Stage, Movies, TV, Books, Magazines, Newspapers, Games, Anime, Manga, Entertainment
- **Health & Wellness** (健康与保健) — Sports, Martial Arts, Exercise, Nutrition, Medical Tech, Rehab, Medicine, Caregiving, Medical Care, Medical Support
- **Travel & Lifestyle** (旅行与生活方式) — Travel, Tourism, Theme Parks, Hotels, Fashion, Beauty, Relaxation, Stationery, Leisure, Toys, Tobacco, Lifestyle
- **Relationships** (人际关系) — Weddings, Funerals, Sexuality, Family, Romance, Marriage
- **Design & Engineering** (设计与工程) — Cars, Planes, Motorcycles, Ships, Trains, Architecture, Civil Engineering, Interior Design, Transport
- **Food & Drink** (餐饮) — Cooking, Sweets, Alcohol, Food, Food & Drink
- **Education & Social** (教育与社会) — Social Welfare, Education, Childcare, Language Learning, International, Psychology, Chemistry, Philosophy, Religion
- **Law & Politics** (法律与政治) — Politics, Law, Security, Administration
- **Business & Finance** (商业与金融) — Finance, Business, Career, Management, Real Estate, Advertising, Marketing, Economics, Consulting, Office Work, Service Industry, Logistics, Sales
