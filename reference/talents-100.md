# Reference — 100 Talents (each with its strength & shadow)

Use this in Stage 2 ([`../method/02-talents.md`](../method/02-talents.md)) as a controlled vocabulary for naming talents. The point of the **shadow** column: every talent, overused or stressed, shows up as a weakness — naming *both* sides is how the user recognizes the talent as truly theirs (and it's more honest than flattery).

Official source: Yagi Jinpei, 『世界一やさしい「やりたいこと」の見つけ方』巻末特典「例100リスト」 (Japanese original). The **Talent** column is the official 才能 name; **Strength** is 長所使い; **Shadow** is 短所使い. EN and ZH are faithful translations, updated 2026-11-11. IDs 1–100 are unchanged.

> EN **Strength** / 中文 **长处** translate the book's 長所使い; EN **Shadow** / 中文 **短处** translate 短所使い. The printed #58 短所使い reads 「犠牲にしまう」 (intended 犠牲にしてしまう); EN/ZH use the intended wording. Joined phrases with ／ at #50, #67, and #91 come from line breaks in the printed appendix.
