# Reference — 100 Values + Trap Values

Use this as a **controlled vocabulary** in Stage 1 ([`../method/01-values.md`](../method/01-values.md)): map the user's value-keywords onto these so the output stays consistent and method-aligned. It's an anchor, not a cage — if the user's truest word isn't here, keep their word.

Official source: Yagi Jinpei, 『世界一やさしい「やりたいこと」の見つけ方』巻末特典「例100リスト」 (Japanese original). EN and ZH below are faithful translations of the official Japanese label and meaning, updated 2026-11-11. IDs 1–100 are unchanged.

## Trap values — probe these instead of accepting them

A trap value is a surface want that **depends on others' reactions** or is **just a means to something deeper**. When one shows up in the user's top values, don't take it at face value — ask the probe and help them name what's underneath.

| Trap value | JP | ZH | Probe question | Likely deeper value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wealth** | 裕福 | 富裕 | "Money is a tool. Having it lets you feel — what?" | Freedom · Peace of Mind · Comfort · Independence |
| **Abundance** | 余裕 | 宽裕 | "What does abundance protect you from? Is it Peace of Mind?" | Peace of Mind · Freedom |
| **Fame** | 名声 | 名声 | "Fame depends on others. If you were famous, what would that let you do?" | Influence · Expression |
| **Popularity** | 人気 | 人气 | "Popularity is being liked. What do you get from being liked?" | Acceptance · Peace of Mind · Loving |
| **Being Loved** | 愛される | 被爱 | "'Being loved' is passive. What active value do you want to create?" | Loving · Intimacy |
| **Victory** | 勝利 | 胜利 | "Victory is fleeting. What do you enjoy *during* the competition?" | Growth · Mastery · Challenge |
| **Achievement** | 達成 | 达成 | "After you achieve the goal, how do you want to feel?" | Contribution · Growth · Purpose |
| **Authority** | 権力 | 权力 | "We can't control others. What does authority let you exercise?" | Responsibility · Influence · Service |

The pattern: **a value that depends on other people's reactions, or is only a means to something else, is a trap — dig one level down.**

The previous off-list probe name **Correctness** (and the matching Accuracy probe) has been retired. Use the official list item **Correctness** / 正確性 / 正确性 as an ordinary value, in the sense of conveying one's views correctly — not as “fear of being wrong.”
